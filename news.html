Oct 3, 2026

Meta opens Muse AI to hardware makers, ships Muse Home Link

Meta open-sourced Muse Gadgets — ESP32 firmware, a Linux SDK, and docs under Apache 2.0 — so developers can build custom peripherals (screens, buttons, sensors) on off-the-shelf boards. Its own Muse Home Link, a USB-C gadget that lets Muse control smart TVs and speakers, is going free to 5,000 active US subscribers. — TheJOAI

Oct 3, 2026

Meta launches Muse shopping agent that checks out on partner sites for a fee

Muse can now browse and complete checkouts across partner retailers — Walmart, Gap, Sephora — with Meta taking a small fee per transaction and an approval card before every charge. Amazon has blocked Muse from buying on its site, citing credential-storage and scraping concerns. — Complete AI Training

Oct 3, 2026

BBC’s AI Decoded asks: Meta’s Muse — cute AI agent or “AI Trojan horse”?

Christian Fraser hosts Ed Zitron and Ben Hatton debating whether Muse is a helpful personal agent or a data-hungry trojan horse — with the panel split on whether the critics have actually used the product. — BBC News / YouTube

Oct 1, 2026

Meta plans AI glasses integration and expands its consumer agent

Announced at Connect: Muse comes to AI glasses within months — activate by name, act on what you see — plus improved voice, more service connections (Instacart, Notion, GitHub, Box), a dedicated email address, and Muse Charm, a pocket-sized device. — AI Market Watch

Sep 29, 2026

OpenAI answers with Dots AI, aimed at Pro and business users

OpenAI announced Dots AI, its rival personal agent, targeting Pro and business users — while Sensor Tower estimates put Muse at ~5M downloads in the US and Canada within 22 days of launch, faster than ChatGPT’s early climb. — OpenAIMaster

Sep 21, 2026

Researcher finds macOS zero-day in Muse; Meta hotfixes in ~12 hours

Patrick Wardle (Objective-See) disclosed an undocumented macOS dictation setting that let any local process redirect Muse’s dictation traffic and steal account tokens — no remote exploit needed. Meta removed the setting from production builds about 12 hours after publication. — DeafNews / Ars Technica
